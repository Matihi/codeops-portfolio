About US

At Habesha Eatery, we celebrate the rich flavors, traditions, and hospitality of Ethiopian cuisine. Our restaurant brings together authentic recipes, fresh ingredients, and traditional cooking techniques to create a dining experience that reflects the heart of Ethiopia. Every dish is carefully prepared to showcase the unique spices, aromas, and flavors that make Ethiopian food truly special.
From shared platters of injera and flavorful stews to a variety of vegetarian and meat specialties, our menu is inspired by generations of culinary heritage. We believe that Ethiopian dining is more than just food, it is a way of bringing people together, encouraging connection, conversation, and unforgettable moments around the table.
At Habesha Eatery, our mission is to provide a welcoming place where guests can enjoy authentic Ethiopian flavors with exceptional service and a warm atmosphere. Whether you are discovering Ethiopian cuisine for the first time or returning for your favorite dishes, we invite you to experience the culture, tradition, and taste of Ethiopia with us.
